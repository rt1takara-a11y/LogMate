---
name: Logmate insight evidence
description: How to keep local text-derived insights honest and compatible with old journal history.
---

Treat locally inferred themes, possible unresolved issues, and Direction comparisons as prompts for the owner to inspect, not confirmed facts. A later positive-sounding phrase cannot reliably close an earlier issue; negation and broad shared subjects can make evidence look more conclusive than it is.

**Why:** Japanese shop notes can use varied wording, negations, and overlapping general topics. Overly broad grouping or automatic resolution hides real problems and produces misleading counts or manual recommendations.

**How to apply:** Keep representative source text visible and distinguish explicit user evaluations from inferred relevance. Use narrow thematic overlap for repeated-issue recommendations. Time-limit weekly and trend views, but retain eligible manual suggestions from the full local history so old useful entries do not disappear.
